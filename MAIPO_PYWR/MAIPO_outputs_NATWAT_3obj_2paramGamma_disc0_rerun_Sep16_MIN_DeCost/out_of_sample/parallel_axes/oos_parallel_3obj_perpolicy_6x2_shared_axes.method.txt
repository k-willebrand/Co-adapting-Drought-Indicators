OOS parallel-axis method

Policy run: sep16_decost

Each line represents a policy retained on an indicator-period-specific OOS
Pareto set. For every policy, total cost, urban reliability, and agricultural
reliability are first averaged across all 108 OOS climate scenarios. The OOS
Pareto mask minimizes mean total cost and maximizes both mean reliabilities.
No temperature, precipitation, glacier-melt, streamflow, or other climate
subgroup is used. Total cost is shown in M$ and its normalized parallel axis is
reversed so that better performance is upward on all three axes. The plotting
workflow reads completed caches and does not rerun Pywr or Borg.
When requested, the additional 6x2 per-policy grid uses one row per drought
indicator and one column per period. All 12 panels share the global OOS-Pareto
cost range; urban reliability is displayed from 0.7 to 1.0 and agricultural
reliability from 0.290 to 0.611. Values outside those requested display bounds are
reported in the companion scale JSON and clipped only in the figure.
