Period-B SPI/SRI classification with a mean-cost cap

The source policies and scenario performance come from the completed Sep16
Period-B OOS caches. For each indicator, a policy is eligible only when its
total cost averaged across all 108 OOS scenarios is no greater than
5000 M$. This eligibility set is fixed before scenarios
are classified.

For each scenario and indicator, the eligible policy with the highest urban
reliability is selected. Reliability ties are resolved deterministically by
lower scenario-specific total cost, higher agricultural reliability, the
lexicographically smaller decision vector, and source policy index. The largest
urban reliability among SPI3/SPI6/SPI12 is then compared directly with the
largest among SRI3/SRI6/SRI12. Only a strictly larger family value produces an
SPI or SRI assignment. Exact equality is recorded as Tie and excluded from the
violin panels.

Temperature, precipitation, glacier-melt, and streamflow percentile ranks use
the same final-20-year metrics and name-based scenario matching as the original
Sep16 violin analysis. No Pywr simulation is rerun and no scenario is dropped
from the classification table.
