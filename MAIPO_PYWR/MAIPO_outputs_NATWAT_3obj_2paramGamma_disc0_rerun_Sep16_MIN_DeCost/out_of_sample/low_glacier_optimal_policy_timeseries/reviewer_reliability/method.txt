Sep16 low-glacier reliability time-series method

The ten scenarios, SPI12/SRI12 policies, and deterministic tie rules are identical to the existing highest-urban-reliability low-glacier workflow. Six fixed-policy simulations evaluate all 108 OOS scenarios: no policy, SPI12, and SRI12 in each period. No optimization is rerun. The no-policy counterfactual has zero contract purchases and retains 100% of unrestricted urban demand. Sep16 simulations explicitly use $1/m3 curtailed-demand cost and a minimum demand-retention factor of 0.7.

Panel a overlays both periods on elapsed time. Weekly precipitation is summed by calendar month; glacier melt is averaged by calendar month. Panels b and c show SPI12 and SRI12 plus cumulative urban reliability for no policy, the SPI12 policy, and the SRI12 policy. An action-active week is the union of positive contract action and a demand-restriction factor below one. Trigger-points and trigger-fill figures use the same underlying weeks. Panels d and e retain the existing cumulative contract and demand-restriction bars. Weekly failure and cumulative reliability use the formal restricted-demand definition, and every final cumulative value is required to match reliability_PT1.
